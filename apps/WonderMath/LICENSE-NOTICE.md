# WonderMath content and license notice

WonderMath includes a filtered derivative of the Marble Skill Taxonomy (v1).
The bundled taxonomy JSON is generated by `tools/build_taxonomy_subset.py`
from the source taxonomy and research records. The dataset remains open under
the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0, and the associated
content remains available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International (CC BY-SA 4.0).
